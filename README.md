# AI Studio
### One Workspace. Multiple AI Minds. Endless Possibilities.

**AI Studio is a modular, multi-AI workspace designed to bring artificial intelligence, project management, productivity, automation, and personal tools together in one unified environment.**

Instead of switching between different AI platforms, applications, and dashboards, AI Studio gives users a central place to work with multiple AI models, manage projects, organize knowledge, track expenses, and turn ideas into actionable plans.

Built with flexibility, customization, and user control at its core, AI Studio aims to be more than another AI chatbot.

**It is a workspace where artificial intelligence becomes part of your entire workflow.**

---

## 🧠 Intelligence — Multiple AI Minds, One Workspace

Intelligence is the heart of AI Studio.

It brings together AI models from **OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini), and Anthropic (Claude)** in a single interface.

Users can choose which AI providers and models to enable and how they should collaborate.

### Three Collaboration Modes

- **Simultaneous:** Multiple AI models respond independently to the same prompt.
- **Discussion:** AI models contribute different perspectives to a shared discussion.
- **Relay:** One AI builds upon the work of another, creating a collaborative workflow.

Intelligence also provides Grid, List, and Focus layouts, allowing users to work with multiple AI models or concentrate on one conversation at a time.

Additional capabilities include:

- Project-based conversations and context.
- Custom AI skills and project-specific instructions.
- Prompt attachments and file handling.
- Markdown and code formatting.
- Sending responses between AI models.
- Saving AI-generated content to project folders.
- Response archiving and backups.
- Token usage and estimated API cost tracking.
- Configurable AI models and usage limits.

**The goal is not simply to receive answers, but to combine different AI perspectives to produce better results.**

---

## 🧩 Projects & Knowledge Management

AI Studio organizes work around projects rather than isolated conversations.

Each project can contain its own folders, assets, notes, AI conversations, and instructions.

Projects support a configurable **Master Skill**, providing persistent guidance about the project's purpose, requirements, and working methods.

AI Studio also explores contextual memory and token optimization, helping models work with relevant project information more efficiently.

Optional Google Drive integration is designed to support project backups and connected workflows.

---

## 🧠 RoadmapBuilder — Turn Ideas Into Progress

RoadmapBuilder transforms project planning into an interactive visual experience.

At its center is a brain-inspired mind map where ideas grow into categories, tasks, subtasks, and milestones.

### Multiple Views

- **Mindmap:** Explore project ideas and their relationships through visual branches.
- **Kanban:** Organize work into Planned, In Progress, and Completed.
- **Timeline:** Follow project progress through a visual journey, including experimental gamified interactions.

Tasks support hierarchical organization, status tracking, descriptions, and interactive editing.

RoadmapBuilder is also designed to connect with Intelligence, allowing users to ask AI models for suggestions and transfer approved ideas into their roadmaps.

### Achievements & Motivation

RoadmapBuilder includes a developing achievement system with experience points, levels, milestones, and optional rewards.

The aim is to make progress visible and rewarding without forcing users into rigid productivity systems.

This approach may be particularly useful for students, creative thinkers, and people who prefer visual, step-by-step project planning.

---

## 💰 BudgetMaster — Financial Awareness and Planning

BudgetMaster provides a dedicated environment for personal and project-related financial management.

Its features include:

- Income and expense tracking.
- Budget categories and spending limits.
- Recurring expenses.
- Financial summaries and dashboards.
- Budget planning and analysis.
- Visual spending charts.
- Transaction management.

BudgetMaster is designed to integrate with other AI Studio modules, creating opportunities to connect project plans with estimated and actual spending.

---

## 🪙 AI Usage & Cost Monitoring

Running multiple AI models should not mean losing control over API costs.

AI Studio includes a dedicated usage monitoring system for tracking:

- AI requests and token consumption.
- Estimated costs by provider and model.
- Input and output token pricing.
- Weekly and monthly spending.
- Configurable provider budgets.
- USD and NOK currency display.

The system supports automatic model price references alongside manual overrides.

This helps users understand how their AI usage affects their budgets.

---

## 🤖 Jarvis — Your Personal Platform Assistant

Jarvis is AI Studio's integrated platform assistant.

Designed as a floating assistant accessible throughout the workspace, Jarvis aims to help users understand settings, navigate features, and interact with connected services.

Its developing capabilities include:

- Local AI model support.
- Platform assistance and guidance.
- System settings navigation.
- Connected service controls.
- Spotify playback integration.
- Guided setup and configuration.

Jarvis is intended to become a central point of interaction between the user and AI Studio's different modules.

---

## 🏠 HomeControll — Connected Living

HomeControll is AI Studio's smart-home workspace.

It is designed to bring compatible smart-home services into a unified interface with support for connected devices, homes, and integrations.

The long-term vision includes Google Home, Home Assistant, Apple Home, and Amazon Alexa integrations, where supported by their respective APIs and platforms.

The interface is designed around simple device management, room organization, and convenient access to connected services.

Some integrations remain experimental or planned.

---

## 📊 Customizable Dashboard

AI Studio includes a personalized dashboard with movable and configurable widgets.

Users can arrange information according to their preferences, adjust widget sizes, and hide unnecessary tools.

Dashboard components include areas such as:

- QuickCalendar.
- Weather information.
- BudgetMaster summaries.
- AI usage monitoring.
- Notes and productivity tools.
- System information.
- Time and date.

The modular design lets users build a workspace that matches how they prefer to work.

---

## 📝 Notes, History & Integrations

AI Studio combines productivity tools with its AI workspace.

Features include Markdown-capable notes, conversation history, project file management, searchable content, and integrations with external services.

The platform also explores calendar synchronization, media controls, cloud storage, and local AI workflows.

---

## 🔐 Privacy, Modularity & User Control

AI Studio is designed with self-hosting and configurable integrations in mind.

Users can choose which major modules they want enabled, including Intelligence, HomeControll, BudgetMaster, and RoadmapBuilder.

The goal is to give users more control over their working environment, their AI providers, and their data.

AI Studio's architecture also supports future development toward more flexible deployment and local-first workflows.

**Privacy and security remain active development priorities.** The Alpha version should not be considered a security-audited production deployment.

---

## 🖥️ Technology

AI Studio Flat currently uses a lightweight web-based architecture:

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- **Backend:** Python.
- **Database:** SQLite.
- **AI integrations:** Provider-specific APIs.
- **Storage:** Local project data with optional external integrations.
- **Interface:** Responsive, modular, dark-themed workspace.

The architecture is being developed with local installation, browser access, and future self-hosted deployments in mind.

## 🚀 Demo & Downloads

A public demo edition is planned to allow visitors to explore the interface without needing to create an account or configure external services.

The planned demo will include:

- A preconfigured demonstration user.
- Sample projects and roadmap data.
- Example AI conversations.
- Interactive interface previews.

## ⚠️ Development Status

**AI Studio is currently in active Alpha development.**

Many features are functional and undergoing testing, while others remain experimental, incomplete, or part of the longer-term roadmap.

Expect changes to interfaces, capabilities, integrations, and deployment requirements as development continues.

The public demo may not represent every feature available in the downloadable development edition.
